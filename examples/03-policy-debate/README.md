# 03 · Policy Debate

**Goal:** a genuine multi-model deliberation on a real question — opposed
consultants, multiple rounds, a synthesized verdict, a mechanical guard against
mush, and an executive distillation.

**Input:** "Should a 40-person software company adopt a 4-day work week?"

| # | Step | Type | What it does | Expected output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Council deliberation | council | Chair frames; Advocate vs Skeptic debate 2 rounds (different models); Rapporteur synthesizes | A verdict containing the word RECOMMENDATION, with adoption conditions, risks, and a trial design |
| 2 | Verdict guard | condition | Output must contain an explicit RECOMMENDATION or the pipeline stops rather than shipping mush | continue |
| 3 | Executive summary | agent | Small model compresses the deliberation | ≤120-word paragraph + exactly 5 bullets: verdict, conditions, top risk, success metric, review date |
