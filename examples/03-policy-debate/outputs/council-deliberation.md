**Limited‑Team 4‑Day‑Week Pilot – Work Directive**

RECOMMENDATION

**Title & Purpose**  
Limited‑Team 4‑Day‑Week Pilot – Work Directive  
Test a 32‑hour, 4‑day work week for 10 weeks while measuring impact on velocity, defect rate, employee wellbeing, and SLA compliance.

**Scope**  
- **Pilot population:** One cross‑functional product squad of ~8‑12 people (e.g., a feature team delivering a core product line) that performs typical development, testing, and delivery work.  
- **Out of scope:** Support, sales, operations, or any role that cannot compress hours into Monday‑Thursday; those roles will receive equivalent flex‑days or compensated time‑off as noted in the risk mitigation table.

**Timeline & Schedule**  
- **Start date:** Monday 2 Nov 2025  
- **Duration:** 10 weeks (≈2.5 months), running Monday‑Thursday, 8 hours/day (32 hours/week).  
- **Protected Friday:** No regular work; optional work allowed only for emergencies and must be logged.  
- **Key checkpoints:** End of week 2, week 5, and week 8 – brief pulse surveys & metric review; final review at week 10.

**Baseline Data Collection (pre‑pilot)**  
- **Metrics to capture over the 6‑sprint baseline:**  
  - Average story points completed per sprint (velocity)  
  - Defects found in production per sprint  
  - eNPS from pulse survey  
  - Overtime % of total logged hours  
  - SLA compliance %  
  - CSAT/NPS  
  - % of employees logging any work on Friday  
- **Source systems:**  
  - Jira – story points & defect counts  
  - HRIS – overtime hours  
  - SurveyMonkey – eNPS pulse survey  
  - Monitoring/dashboard – SLA compliance & CSAT/NPS  
- **Baseline completion deadline:** EOD Friday 31 Oct 2025  
- **Responsible:** Scrum Master + Data Analyst (joint ownership)

**Success Criteria (Acceptance Criteria)**  
1. Velocity change ≤ ±5 % vs. baseline.  
2. Defect leakage change ≤ ±5 % vs. baseline.  
3. eNPS increase ≥ 5 points.  
4. Overtime ≤ 2 % of total logged hours.  
5. SLA compliance ≥ 95 % and CSAT/NPS unchanged or improved.  
6. ≤ 10 % of employees log any work on protected Friday.  

*All six criteria must be met to proceed to the company‑wide phase; any failure triggers a rollback to the 5‑day week, a retrospective, and a decision on next steps.*

**Mitigation Actions (aligned with the risk table)**  
- **SLA compliance ≥ 95 % and CSAT/NPS unchanged or improved.**  
- **Inequity risk:** Offer equivalent flex‑days or compensated time‑off for roles unable to compress hours; communicate plan transparently in kickoff meeting.  
- **Scope creep / focus loss:** Keep sprint goals unchanged; monitor velocity; enforce Definition of Done; review sprint backlog at each planning.  
- **Coordination loss:** Replace ad‑hoc chats with structured async updates (written stand‑ups, recorded demos); hold a weekly 30‑minute video sync for the squad.  
- **Legal/compliance:** HR/legal review completed before start; confirm salaried exempt status or adjust pay to keep total compensation constant.  
- **Trial fatigue:** Fixed 10‑week length; weekly pulse surveys; celebrate milestones (e.g., end‑of‑week 5 recognition).

**Roles & Responsibilities**  
- **Pilot Lead (e.g., Engineering Manager):** overall coordination, ensures schedule adherence, chairs weekly sync.  
- **Scrum Master (per squad):** facilitates sprint ceremonies, tracks velocity/defect metrics, runs retrospectives.  
- **Data Analyst:** extracts baseline and pilot metrics, prepares weekly metric dashboard.  
- **HR Partner:** oversees legal/compliance check, manages flex‑day arrangements for excluded roles, runs eNPS surveys.  
- **Product Owner:** prioritizes backlog, ensures sprint goals stay constant, communicates any client‑impact concerns.  
- **Team Members:** log hours honestly, report any Friday work, complete pulse surveys, follow async communication norms.

**Communication Plan**  
- **Kickoff meeting (day 0):** present directive, answer questions, distribute calendar invites for protected Fridays.  
- **Weekly async update:** written stand‑up posted in team channel by 10 am Monday; recorded demo shared Friday afternoon (optional).  
- **Weekly 30‑minute video sync** (same day/time each week): review metrics, blockers, and any SLA incidents.  
- **Bi‑weekly metric dashboard** shared with stakeholders (HR, legal, execs).  
- **End‑of‑pilot review meeting:** present final metric comparison against criteria, decide on next phase.

**Deliverables & Artefacts**  
- Baseline metrics report (pre‑pilot).  
- Weekly metrics snapshots (velocity, defects, overtime, SLA, Friday work %).  
- Pulse survey results (weeks 2, 5, 8, 10).  
- Final pilot report (including recommendation to proceed or rollback).  
- Retrospective notes and action items (if rollback occurs).

**Definition of “Done” for This Directive**  
The directive is considered complete when it contains all numbered sections above, is written in clear prose, and can be handed directly to the pilot lead for execution without further clarification.
